what attainable means

Attainable doesn't mean cheap. It means the price reflects the product, not the route it took to reach you.

We work on a factory-direct model. Each item is carefully crafted with trusted, tested manufacturing partners, at the scale of a house of brands. What that saves goes into the material, not the middle man.

No markup for the logo on the box.
